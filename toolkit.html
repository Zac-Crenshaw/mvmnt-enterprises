Reading a receipt

Look for these signals when you're out. They tell you if there's an opportunity before you even say a word.

Price matches the tag exactly
No fee line item — they're absorbing the cost themselves. This is your opening.
No cash discount offered
If there's no cash vs. card price difference, they're eating every swipe.
No "non-cash adjustment" line
This is what a cash discount program looks like on a receipt — if it's missing, they're not running one.
Fee line item present
Shows "Credit Card Fee", "Processing Fee", or "Non-Cash Adjustment" — they're already passing fees. Smaller opportunity or upgrade conversation.
Two prices listed
"Cash price" vs "Card price" — they have a cash discount program running already.

Your opening line

"Hey I was just in here — noticed you're not passing card fees to customers. Are you aware that's costing you money every month? I can show you how to flip that."
